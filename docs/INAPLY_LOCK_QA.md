# Inaply Lock — Hardware QA Checklist

Physical open-frame anti-theft & anti-tamper enclosure for Android TV / Google TV dongles.

## 1. Thermal Validation
- [ ] Continuous 4K/1080p video playback + Wi-Fi casting for 4 hours at 30°C ambient room temperature.
- [ ] SoC temperature remains below thermal throttling threshold (< 75°C).
- [ ] Passive ventilation slots unobstructed on top and bottom sides of the enclosure.

## 2. Mechanical & Anti-Tamper Validation
- [ ] Security Torx / tamper-resistant screws lock the dongle and HDMI pigtail inside the frame.
- [ ] VESA / behind-TV bracket mount holds ≥ 2 kg static load without flexing.
- [ ] USB-C power & HDMI ports are strain-relieved and cannot be unplugged without unlocking the enclosure.
- [ ] Wi-Fi / Bluetooth RF attenuation < 2 dBm compared to unenclosed dongle.
